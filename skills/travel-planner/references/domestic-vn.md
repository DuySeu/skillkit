# Vietnam domestic travel reference

Read this before Step 3 when the trip is **inside Vietnam**.

## Booking platforms

| Platform | Best for |
|----------|----------|
| Agoda | Hotels, resorts, deals in VN |
| Traveloka | Domestic promos, bundles |
| Booking.com | International chains, cancellation policies |
| Airbnb | Villa, homestay, whole-place stays |
| Google Maps | Real ratings, photos, open hours, distance |

Always note **which platform** a price came from and the **search date**.

## Transport defaults

Ask explicitly — do not assume.

| Mode | Cost hints |
|------|------------|
| Self-drive car | Fuel + toll (QL, CT) + parking; ~10–20% of trip budget for weekend from Hanoi/HCMC |
| Grab / taxi | Per leg; surge on holidays |
| Limousine / bus | Per seat; book early for Tet/weekends |
| Motorbike rental | Daily rate + deposit; helmet included? |

Include **departure city** in distance estimates (e.g. "80 km from Hanoi").

## Money

- Quote in **VND** unless user asks otherwise
- Weekend and holiday rates can be 1.5–2× weekday
- "Giá tham khảo tại [ngày]" on every lodging price

## Search queries (WebSearch)

- `[địa điểm] resort homestay [tháng] giá`
- `[tên chỗ] review google maps`
- `thời tiết [địa điểm] [tháng/năm]`

## Regional notes

- **Northern mountains** (Sapa, Mộc Châu, Tam Đảo): cool nights; check fog/rain in summer
- **Central coast** (Đà Nẵng, Hội An): typhoon season Sep–Nov
- **South** (Phú Quốc, Vũng Tàu): dry season Nov–Apr popular

## Cancellation

Prefer **free cancellation** or flexible dates when user is unsure — call it out in pros/cons.
