# TechX defaults — proposal cover & boilerplate

Use unless the user overrides. Do not copy a prior customer's name into a new proposal.

## Cover

- **Prepared by:** TechX AI Team
- **Date:** use engagement date from user, or `(dd/mm/yyyy)` until confirmed

## Title pattern

`[Tên dự án] Proposal ([POC/Production])`

Examples:

- `Nền tảng GenAI Hải quan Proposal (POC)`
- `Document Intelligence Proposal (Production)`

## Partner positioning (1.1 Bối cảnh — optional phrases)

- TechX is an AWS Premier Tier Services Partner with GenAI and enterprise
  transformation experience.
- Solutions are delivered on AWS managed services with measurable acceptance
  criteria agreed before evaluation phases.

## Assumption starters (section 7)

- Customer provides sample data / documents in agreed format before build phase.
- Measurement thresholds are agreed in writing before the evaluation phase.
- Infrastructure access, quotas, and technical integration contacts are ready.
- Customer business SMEs participate in UAT and threshold confirmation.

## Out-of-scope starters (section 8)

- Production HA/DR and core-system integration outside agreed POC scope.
- Work not listed in section 1.3 OUT OF SCOPE table.
- Quality commitments beyond the agreed sample set and signed thresholds.
